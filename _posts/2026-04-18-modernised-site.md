We shipped a rebuilt monetago.com today. The short version:

- Information architecture now runs **by audience** (banks and NBFIs, fintechs and factors, public sector) rather than by product brochure order.
- Copy is **verbs and numbers**. Where we used to say "world's foremost provider", we now name the institutions, the pilot cohort size, and the Swift API go-live date.
- The site is a static Jekyll build on GitHub Pages. A single data file controls the customer strip; anyone on the team can add a news post in five minutes.

## What changed

### Navigation

The old site grouped content by product and technology. That is how we think about the product internally. It is not how a trade-risk officer at a bank, a credit head at a factoring platform, or a regulator at a central bank thinks about their problem.

The new primary nav is:

1. **Solutions** — three hubs keyed to buyer type.
2. **Platform** — the Secure Financing modules.
3. **How it works** — the data flow in one diagram.
4. **Evidence** — customers, quotes, numbers, case studies.
5. **Resources, Insights, About.**

### Copy

Every page opens with a one-sentence claim a reader can repeat verbatim. Adjectives either have a number or a citation next to them, or they are deleted. No "leading". No "empowering". No "trusted".

### Visual

Institutional navy, slate, warm off-white, a single restrained teal accent. Source Serif 4 for headings, Inter for everything else. No stock photography; all diagrams are SVG.

## What this site is not

- It is not a lead-capture funnel. You can email us from any page.
- It is not an SEO content farm. No listicles, no "top 10 trade-finance trends".
- It is not a marketing brochure. It is the evidence file.
