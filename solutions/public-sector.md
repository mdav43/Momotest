## Who this is for

- **Central banks and regulators** responsible for financial stability and trade integrity.
- **Trade bodies and standard-setters** coordinating industry responses to cross-border fraud.
- **Export credit agencies** and sovereign-backed trade financiers.

## What Digital Public Infrastructure means here

A registry is DPI when it is:

- **Neutral** — run as shared infrastructure, not by any single commercial participant.
- **Standards-aligned** — interoperable with Swift, ICC digital trade standards, and national rails.
- **Auditable** — with privacy-preserving evidence that fraud signals are generated as described.

MonetaGo's Secure Financing registry meets all three. It is the first third-party trade service on Swift and underpins ICC UK's "Shutting fraudsters out of trade" workstream.
