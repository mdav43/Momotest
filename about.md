## What we do

We operate Secure Financing — a cross-institution registry used by banks, non-bank financial institutions, fintechs, factoring companies, and trade-finance funds to prevent duplicate financing and authenticate trade documents. The service is live on the Swift API and underpins industry initiatives led by ICC United Kingdom's Centre for Digital Trade and Innovation.

## How we're governed

- Registry operation is segregated from any single commercial participant.
- Confidential-computing architecture is independently reviewable.
- We publish what we can about participation and aggregate matches, and we speak on the record about what we can't.
