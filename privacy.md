This page covers only the public marketing site at monetago.com. Product data handling is described separately in the platform and technical-brief documentation under [Resources]({{ '/resources/' | relative_url }}).

## What we collect

- **Server logs** — standard web-server logs (IP, user-agent, timestamp, requested path) retained for 30 days for security and operations.
- **Email** — if you write to us, we keep that correspondence in our CRM.
- **No analytics cookies** — we do not currently use Google Analytics or similar.

## What we do with it

- Respond to your enquiries.
- Investigate abuse or security incidents.
- Comply with legal obligations.
